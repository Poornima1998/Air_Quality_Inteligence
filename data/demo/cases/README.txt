DEMONSTRATION CASES
===================

Upload ONLY these files to Streamlit:
  - typical_case_input.csv
  - largest_error_case_input.csv

Do NOT upload case_references.csv or case_references.json. They contain
the actual benzene target and the recorded ANN prediction for comparison
after you have generated the forecast in the web app.

For the incorrect-prediction demonstration, upload largest_error_case_input.csv,
generate the forecast, then compare it against largest_error_case in
case_references.csv.
